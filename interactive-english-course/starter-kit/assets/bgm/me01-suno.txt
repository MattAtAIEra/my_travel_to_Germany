Suno 背景音樂提示詞：me01　The Elevator

【Style of Music】
soft lo-fi morning jazz, mellow electric piano, brushed drums, warm upright bass, calm office morning, 80 bpm, unobtrusive background, instrumental, no vocals

【Title】
Eleven Floors

【Song Description（簡單模式才用）】
A loopable instrumental background track for "The Elevator", an English learning episode for language learners. Style: soft lo-fi morning jazz, mellow electric piano, brushed drums, warm upright bass, calm office morning, 80 bpm, unobtrusive background, instrumental, no vocals. Keep it steady and soft so spoken voices stay clear.

做法：
1. 登入 suno.com，按 Create，切到 Custom，打開 Instrumental。
2. 把上面的 Style of Music 和 Title 貼進去，按 Create（一次會有兩首）。
3. 挑最不搶戲的那一首：旋律簡單、沒有突然的大鼓或高音。
4. 按 ⋯ → Download → MP3 Audio，改名成 me01.mp3，放回這個資料夾（assets/bgm/me01.mp3）。
5. 打開 studio.html →「⑥ 背景音樂」測音量，把數字寫進題庫的 bgm.volume。
