Suno 背景音樂提示詞：kk01　Two Apples

【Style of Music】
cheerful marimba and ukulele, light hand claps, bouncy children's cartoon, warm kitchen morning, 100 bpm, simple happy melody, instrumental, no vocals

【Title】
Two Apples Kitchen

【Song Description（簡單模式才用）】
A loopable instrumental background track for "Two Apples", an English learning episode for language learners. Style: cheerful marimba and ukulele, light hand claps, bouncy children's cartoon, warm kitchen morning, 100 bpm, simple happy melody, instrumental, no vocals. Keep it steady and soft so spoken voices stay clear.

做法：
1. 登入 suno.com，按 Create，切到 Custom，打開 Instrumental。
2. 把上面的 Style of Music 和 Title 貼進去，按 Create（一次會有兩首）。
3. 挑最不搶戲的那一首：旋律簡單、沒有突然的大鼓或高音。
4. 按 ⋯ → Download → MP3 Audio，改名成 kk01.mp3，放回這個資料夾（assets/bgm/kk01.mp3）。
5. 打開 studio.html →「⑥ 背景音樂」測音量，把數字寫進題庫的 bgm.volume。
