# ME01 — The Elevator

**Morning English · B1–B2 · full English immersion**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Situation | 三十秒，電梯裡只有你和經營整間公司的人 |
| Teaching arc | small talk → pitch → follow-up |
| Choice points | 3（每點 3 選項，選完收回主線） |
| Voices | 旁白 `0sGQQaD2G2X1s87kHM5b` ／ LEO `hd5CZt9EmlupFYXziBnV` ／ DIANE `YyqkX0AHv8W5D1vxG9lR` |
| Theme | `manga` |

## Cast

- **LEO** — 玩家視角。22–24 歲，剛畢業，上任 PM 第一週。有想法，還不懂職場分寸。
- **DIANE WARD** — 中年資深女性主管，經營整間公司。和藹、溫暖、好親近。
  話不多，每句都有份量，但份量來自見識而不是威嚇。她是真心想聽聽這個新人在想什麼。
  **不要把她寫成或畫成刻薄嚴厲的人** —— 學習者每天早上要面對這個角色，
  令人生畏的長輩會讓人下意識不想打開這一集。很有經驗不代表要刻薄。

## 寫作規範

- 一句一個念頭 —— 每句就是一個字幕、一顆 mp3。英文 6–18 字。
- 情境對話 B1–B2；解說用更淺的英文（避免關係子句堆疊）。
- **角色的話一定要獨立成句**。`Diane turns slightly. "So, what are you working on?"`
  寫成一句，她就永遠發不出聲音 —— 旁白歸旁白、台詞歸台詞。
- 每個分支：**LEO 的台詞（`signature`）→ DIANE 的回應 → 三句解說**。
- 解說講**代價與時機**，不講對錯 —— 三個選項都是「對的」答案。
- **DIANE 的回應不可引用其他分支發生過的事**。hub-and-spoke 下 BEAT 3 可能接在
  任何一個 BEAT 2 之後，引用前文一定穿幫。

## s01 · COLD OPEN（`chapter: COLD OPEN`）

先給危機再給身分 —— 履歷式的自我介紹不是鉤子。

```
001  旁白   It's 8:52. You're late.
002  旁白   Six days into your first real job, Leo — and you're already late.
003  旁白   Project manager. At least, that's what the badge says.
004  旁白   The elevator doors are closing. You run.
```

## s02 · The doors close

```
005  旁白   You make it. Perfect.
006  旁白   Except — you're not alone.
007  旁白   Standing right next to you is Diane Ward. She runs the whole company.
008  旁白   Eleven floors. About thirty seconds. Nobody is speaking.
```

## s03 · CHOICE 1 —— 怎麼開口（`chapter: BEAT 1`）

```
009  旁白   The doors close. Thirty seconds.
010  旁白   What do you say?
```

### s04a · `b1a` CASUAL

```
011  LEO  ★ Morning! Big day for the team, huh?
069  DIANE  It is. And you must be the new one.
012  旁白   That's warm, and it's natural.
013  旁白   "Huh?" does the real work. It hands her the turn, and a greeting becomes a conversation.
014  旁白   It's casual for a CEO you've never met. Small bet. This time, it pays off.
```

### s04b · `b1b` FORMAL

```
015  LEO  ★ Good morning, Ms. Ward. It's a pleasure to finally meet you.
068  DIANE  Diane, please. Ms. Ward is my mother.
016  旁白   Safe, polite, correct. Nothing here can go wrong.
017  旁白   But listen to the word "finally" — it quietly says you've been hoping for this.
018  旁白   You just spent five seconds creating distance you now have to close.
```

### s04c · `b1c` SAFE

```
019  LEO  ★ Good morning.
070  DIANE  Good morning.
020  旁白   Two words. Nothing wrong with them.
021  旁白   And nothing memorable either. In a thirty-second window, that spends your turn without buying anything.
022  旁白   She nods. The silence comes back. Now it's heavier.
```

她回敬同樣的空白 —— 對話就此結束。這正是這個選項的代價。

## s05 · BEAT 2 setup（`chapter: BEAT 2`）

**這一場是主線，必須對三條分支都成立。** 早期版本寫成「Diane 微微轉身，
主動問你在忙什麼」，接在 b1c 的「沉默回來了，而且更沉重」之後完全不合理。
改成「她做了一件她不必做的事」—— 對聊開的人是自然延續，對冷場的人是她給的
第二次機會，順便演出她的和藹。

```
023  旁白   Floor six. Seven.
077  旁白   Then she does something she doesn't have to do.
067  DIANE  So — what are you working on?
024  旁白   Four words. This is the door.
025  旁白   You have maybe twenty seconds before floor eleven.
```

## s06 · CHOICE 2 —— 要不要 pitch

```
026  旁白   She asked. Twenty seconds.
027  旁白   What do you do with them?
```

### s07a · `b2a` BOLD

```
028  LEO  ★ I'm building a tool that uses AI to cut our campaign copy from three days to three hours.
071  DIANE  Three hours. Say that again in the meeting on Thursday.
029  旁白   That's a whole pitch in one breath.
030  旁白   Notice the shape: what it does, then the number. She'll remember three days to three hours long after she forgets your name.
031  旁白   Bold works when the number is real. If it isn't, this is the sentence that ends you.
```

### s07b · `b2b` STRATEGIC

```
032  LEO  ★ Can I ask you something first — what slows your marketing team down the most?
072  DIANE  Huh. Nobody's asked me that in a while.
033  旁白   You just gave away your twenty seconds to buy better information.
034  旁白   Can I ask you something first — that asks permission before spending her attention. Senior people notice it.
035  旁白   She may not answer today. But she will remember that you asked instead of pushed.
```

（她不能說「Walk with me」—— 門還沒開，下一場還在電梯裡數樓層。）

### s07c · `b2c` HUMAN

```
036  LEO  ★ Honestly? I'm still learning where the coffee machine is.
073  DIANE  Second floor, past the printers. The good one.
037  旁白   You didn't pitch. You made her laugh at 8:52 in the morning.
038  旁白   That is not nothing. Deals don't close in elevators.
039  旁白   People just decide whether they want the next conversation. She does.
```

040  旁白   Floor nine. Ten.

## s09 · CHOICE 3 —— 怎麼收尾

```
043  旁白   Floor eleven. The doors open.
044  旁白   Last words?
```

### s10a · `b3a` DIRECT

```
045  LEO  ★ Could I get fifteen minutes with you next week?
074  DIANE  Fifteen. Tuesday. Email my assistant.
046  旁白   Direct, and specific.
047  旁白   Fifteen minutes is small enough to say yes to. A meeting is not.
048  旁白   Always name the size of the ask. Vague asks get vague answers.
```

### s10b · `b3b` LIGHT

```
049  LEO  ★ I'll send you one page. If it's boring, you'll never hear from me again.
075  DIANE  One page. I'll read it. And I'll tell you if it's boring.
050  旁白   This is confidence with an escape hatch.
051  旁白   You promised one page, and you gave her permission to ignore it.
052  旁白   Low pressure, high memory. Busy people love a small, cancellable ask.
```

### s10c · `b3c` WARM

```
053  LEO  ★ Thanks for the chat, Diane. Have a good one.
076  DIANE  You too, Leo. My door's not as far as it looks.
054  旁白   Clean, warm, no ask at all.
055  旁白   Sometimes that's exactly right. You're one week in; you don't have to close anything.
056  旁白   But nothing is scheduled. A warm door still needs someone to knock.
```

（她給了溫暖的暗示，所以結尾不能寫「什麼都沒推進」—— 會跟她的話打架。
沒推進的是**行程**，不是關係。）

## s10z · 結局（主線，九條分支共用）

s08 立了「這是她最後會記得的一句話」的懸念。沒有這一場，故事會直接跳進
教學總結，玩家永遠不知道「所以呢」。

```
078  旁白   The doors close behind her.
079  旁白   You're alone in a hallway on the eleventh floor.
080  旁白   Whatever happens next — it started in those thirty seconds.
```

## s11 · RECAP（`chapter: RECAP`）

```
057  旁白   Same elevator. Same thirty seconds. Three different versions of you.
058  [重播 s03 選過的那句]
059  [重播 s06 選過的那句]
060  [重播 s09 選過的那句]
061  旁白   None of them were wrong. They just cost different things.
062  旁白   That's the whole game — knowing what each one costs.
```

`replay` 不產生新音檔，播放器直接放使用者選過那句的 mp3。

## s12 · OUTRO

```
063  旁白   Here's today's challenge.
064  旁白   Find one small window today. A hallway. A coffee line. A call before it starts.
065  旁白   Say the one you didn't pick.
066  旁白   See you tomorrow.
```

## 換一集怎麼改

這份結構就是模板：換情境、換三個選擇點的題材，其餘骨架照抄 ——
`track` 命名（`b1a`–`b3c`）、每分支「台詞 + 對方回應 + 三句解說」、
主線的過場必須對三條分支都成立、結局場景九條共用、RECAP 重播三句、
OUTRO 一句 challenge。
